# Carbon-footprint-
A Python-based tool to calculate and visualize your carbon footprint, with personalized sustainability recommendations. Perfect for individuals or businesses aiming to track and reduce their environmental impact.



## Features ✨
- *Calculate CO2 emissions* from transportation, diet, and energy usage.
- *Interactive CLI* for easy data input.
- *Visualizations* (pie charts, progress trends) using Matplotlib.
- *Personalized tips* to reduce your footprint.
- *Save and track* your progress over time.
